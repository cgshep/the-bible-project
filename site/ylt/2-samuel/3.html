14And David sendeth messengers unto Ish-Bosheth son of Saul, saying, `Give up my wife Michal, whom I betrothed to myself with a hundred foreskins of the Philistines.'

15And Ish-Bosheth sendeth, and taketh her from a man, from Phaltiel son of Laish,
