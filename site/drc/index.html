The Holy Bible

Douay-Rheims Challoner

— ✦ ✝ ✦ —
The Old Testament
Genesis
50 chapters
The book of origins — creation, the fall, the flood, and the patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph.
Exodus
40 chapters
Israel's deliverance from Egypt, the giving of the Law at Sinai, and the construction of the Tabernacle.
Leviticus
27 chapters
Laws of sacrifice, purity, and holiness given to the priestly tribe of Levi.
Numbers
36 chapters
The forty years of wandering in the wilderness, from Sinai to the plains of Moab.
Deuteronomy
34 chapters
Moses' farewell speeches and the renewal of the covenant before entering the Promised Land.
Joshua
24 chapters
The conquest and division of Canaan under the leadership of Joshua.
Judges
21 chapters
The turbulent era of the judges — Deborah, Gideon, Samson — between the conquest and the monarchy.
Ruth
4 chapters
A story of loyalty and redemption — Ruth the Moabitess, great-grandmother of King David.
1 Samuel
31 chapters
From the prophet Samuel through Saul's reign to the rise of David.
2 Samuel
24 chapters
The reign of King David — triumphs, sins, and the promise of an everlasting dynasty.
1 Kings
22 chapters
Solomon's glory, the building of the Temple, the kingdom divided, and the prophet Elijah.
2 Kings
25 chapters
The divided kingdom's decline, the prophet Elisha, and the fall of Israel and Judah.
1 Chronicles
29 chapters
Israel's history retold from Adam to David, with emphasis on worship and the Temple.
2 Chronicles
36 chapters
From Solomon's Temple to the Babylonian exile and Cyrus' decree of return.
Ezra
10 chapters
The return from Babylon and the rebuilding of the Temple under Zerubbabel and Ezra.
Nehemiah
13 chapters
Nehemiah rebuilds the walls of Jerusalem and restores the covenant community.
Esther
16 chapters
Queen Esther saves the Jewish people from destruction in the Persian Empire.
Job
42 chapters
The great poem of innocent suffering — Job's trials, his friends' debate, and God's answer from the whirlwind.
Psalms
150 chapters
The hymnbook of ancient Israel — 150 poems of praise, lament, thanksgiving, and wisdom.
Proverbs
31 chapters
Wisdom literature attributed to Solomon — practical counsel for righteous living.
Ecclesiastes
12 chapters
'Vanity of vanities' — the Preacher's meditation on meaning, time, and the fear of God.
Song of Solomon
8 chapters
A lyric poem celebrating love between bride and bridegroom, read as allegory of divine love.
Isaiah
66 chapters
The greatest of the prophets — visions of judgment, the Suffering Servant, and a new creation.
Jeremiah
52 chapters
The weeping prophet — warnings of exile, the promise of a new covenant, and Jerusalem's fall.
Lamentations
5 chapters
Five poems mourning the destruction of Jerusalem and the Temple in 586 BC.
Ezekiel
48 chapters
Visions from Babylonian exile — the chariot throne, the valley of dry bones, and the restored Temple.
Daniel
14 chapters
Stories of faithfulness in exile and apocalyptic visions of kingdoms and the Son of Man.
Hosea
14 chapters
God's faithful love portrayed through Hosea's marriage to the unfaithful Gomer.
Joel
3 chapters
A plague of locusts as prelude to the Day of the Lord, and the promise of the Spirit poured out.
Amos
9 chapters
A shepherd-prophet denounces social injustice: 'Let justice roll down like waters.'
Obadiah
1 chapter
The shortest book in the Old Testament — a prophecy against Edom.
Jonah
4 chapters
The reluctant prophet, the great fish, and God's mercy extending even to Nineveh.
Micah
7 chapters
Justice, mercy, and humility — 'What doth the Lord require of thee?'
Nahum
3 chapters
A prophecy of the fall of Nineveh, the great Assyrian capital.
Habakkuk
3 chapters
A prophet's dialogue with God about evil and justice — 'the just shall live by faith.'
Zephaniah
3 chapters
The Day of the Lord draws near — judgment and the promise of a humble remnant.
Haggai
2 chapters
A call to rebuild the Temple after the return from Babylonian exile.
Zechariah
14 chapters
Night visions and messianic prophecies accompanying the rebuilding of the Temple.
Malachi
4 chapters
The last Old Testament prophet — a call to faithfulness before the messenger of the covenant.
The New Testament
Matthew
28 chapters
The Gospel for the Jewish audience — Jesus as the promised Messiah, Son of David.
Mark
16 chapters
The earliest Gospel — a swift, vivid portrait of Jesus as the suffering servant.
Luke
24 chapters
The physician's careful account — Jesus as the compassionate saviour of all people.
John
21 chapters
'In the beginning was the Word' — the theological Gospel of signs and discourses.
Acts
28 chapters
The birth of the Church — from Pentecost in Jerusalem to Paul's arrival in Rome.
Romans
16 chapters
Paul's magisterial exposition of the Gospel — justification by faith, life in the Spirit.
1 Corinthians
16 chapters
Paul addresses divisions, ethics, worship, and the resurrection in the Corinthian church.
2 Corinthians
13 chapters
Paul's most personal letter — weakness, suffering, and the treasure in earthen vessels.
Galatians
6 chapters
Freedom in Christ — Paul's passionate defence of justification by faith alone.
Ephesians
6 chapters
The cosmic scope of God's plan — the Church as the body of Christ, the armour of God.
Philippians
4 chapters
A letter of joy from prison — 'Rejoice in the Lord always.'
Colossians
4 chapters
The supremacy of Christ — 'in him all things hold together.'
1 Thessalonians
5 chapters
Encouragement to a young church and teaching on the Lord's return.
2 Thessalonians
3 chapters
Further teaching on the Day of the Lord and a call to steadfastness.
1 Timothy
6 chapters
Paul's pastoral counsel to young Timothy on church order and sound doctrine.
2 Timothy
4 chapters
Paul's final letter — 'I have fought the good fight, I have finished the course.'
Titus
3 chapters
Instructions for church leadership and godly living on the island of Crete.
Philemon
1 chapter
A personal appeal for the runaway slave Onesimus — brotherhood in Christ.
Hebrews
13 chapters
Christ as the great high priest — the superiority of the new covenant over the old.
James
5 chapters
Practical wisdom — 'faith without works is dead.'
1 Peter
5 chapters
Hope in suffering — 'a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ.'
2 Peter
3 chapters
Warnings against false teachers and the certainty of the Lord's return.
1 John
5 chapters
'God is light' and 'God is love' — tests of true fellowship with God.
2 John
1 chapter
A brief letter on truth, love, and discernment against deceivers.
3 John
1 chapter
A personal note commending hospitality and warning against Diotrephes.
Jude
1 chapter
An urgent appeal to 'contend for the faith' against ungodly intruders.
Revelation
22 chapters
The apocalyptic vision of John — the Lamb, the seven seals, and the new Jerusalem.
